Before you choose a robotic actuator

The CRA-RI PRO series integrates the motor, harmonic reducer, encoder and driver in one compact joint module. A few answers up front — the rest we confirm together for your project.

What should I provide for actuator selection?

Your application, continuous and peak torque, speed, operating voltage, size and weight limits, control interface, and quantity. If you already have an exact model and version in mind, include it — we will verify it against your requirements.

How do I check controller compatibility?

Compare the exact motor and controller documentation: protocol version, wiring, supply requirements and supported control modes. Matching interface names alone do not establish compatibility — send us your controller model and we will confirm.

Where do I get technical documents and delivery terms?

Each product page lists the full specification table. Datasheets, manuals and CAD files are available on request — contact us with the exact model, the files you need, quantity and destination for documentation, availability and delivery details.

About Us

Ti5 Robot （钛虎机器人） develops integrated joint actuators that serve as the core motion modules of humanoid robots, robotic arms and precision automation equipment. The CRA-RI PRO series packs a frameless torque motor, harmonic reducer, encoder and servo driver into a single compact unit.

We are an authorized distribution partner of Ti5 Robot, supplying the full CRA-RI PRO series to customers in Europe and North America — with model selection support, technical documentation and after-sales coordination handled directly with the factory.

Tell us your torque, space and interface requirements and we will recommend the right model and configuration for your project.
